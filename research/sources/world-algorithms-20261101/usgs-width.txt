                    <section itemprop="description" id="abstract">
                        <h4 id="abstract_head">Abstract</h4>
                        <div>
                            <div class="abstract-contents">

                                <p>Some hydraulic characteristics of stream channels - depth, width, velocity, and suspended load - are measured quantitatively and vary with discharge as simple power functions at a given river cross section. Similar variations in relation to discharge exist among the cross sections along the length of a river under the condition that discharge at all points is equal in frequency of occurrence. The functions derived for a given cross section and among various cross sections along the river differ only in numerical values of coefficients and exponents.</p>
                            </div>
                        </div>
